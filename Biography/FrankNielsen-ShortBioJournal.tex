Frank Nielsen received his Ph.D. in computer science from the University of C\^ote d’Azur, France, 
for research conducted at INRIA. He is a Fellow at Sony Computer Science Laboratories, Inc. (Japan), 
where his current research focuses on the geometric science of information, 
with particular emphasis on information geometry, computational geometry, and geometric machine learning. 
He has taught visual computing and high-performance computing for data science at \'Ecole Polytechnique, France. 
He currently serves on the editorial boards of Information Geometry (Springer Nature) and Entropy (MDPI)
and co-organizes the biennial conference Geometric Science of Information (GSI).
